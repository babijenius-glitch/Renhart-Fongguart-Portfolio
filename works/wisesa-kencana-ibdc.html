About the Competition

The International Bridge Design Competition (IBDC) at ICEE ITB is one of the most prestigious bridge design competitions for civil engineering students in Indonesia and across the Asia-Pacific region. Teams are challenged to design structurally sound, innovative, and constructible bridge solutions within specified constraints.

Design: Wisesa Kencana Bridge

Wisesa Kencana — meaning "sovereign glory" in Javanese — represents an ambitious bridge concept that pushes the boundaries of structural expression while maintaining rigorous engineering standards. The design was developed to compete at the international level, requiring more sophisticated analysis and presentation than domestic competitions.

Engineering Approach

  • Developed the structural concept and system selection to meet competition-specified loading criteria
  • Performed structural analysis to verify member forces, deflections, and safety factors
  • Optimized SHMS in real case bridge and build an accelerometer for prototype bridge
  • Produced comprehensive design documentation BIM model
  • Prepared the competition submission package with all required deliverables
